# Museum direction

Mode: Experience, with Read for exhibit evidence.

The visitor is inside a contemporary stone museum from the first viewport. Warm ivory limestone, deep olive wayfinding, terracotta objects, softly lit arches and a long gallery establish the world. Cormorant Garamond provides the curatorial voice; Manrope keeps controls legible. The full-screen Three.js canvas is the artifact, not a decorative hero image. Quiet horizontal rules and museum labels replace card grids.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a real rendered gallery visible behind a restrained left introduction, a framed amphora on a plinth in the foreground, repeated architectural bays receding toward a dark green end wall, and an accessible chronological navigation rail below. The introduction has one clear entry action and a presentation alternative.

Signature interaction: walk the gallery, select a real 3D object and bring its evidence into an HTML reading panel. Period selection focuses an exhibit; three local environment models can be entered as explicitly illustrative scenes. Scene changes are cancellable and settle immediately under reduced motion.

Quality bar: real local models, useful controls, no inaccessible canvas-only content, generous type hierarchy, responsive phone reading, source and license fidelity. No external imagery is required: authored Three.js architecture and supplied models are the visual medium.
